⚙️ Technical Methodology

How PhisLens Operates

An end-to-end look at lexical feature extraction, ensemble model classification, and empirical performance metrics.

STEP 01

URL Feature Engineering

When a URL is submitted, PhisLens normalizes the string safely and extracts 26 purely lexical and statistical dimensions without visiting the website:

  • Structural: URL length, path depth, hostname length, and query length.
  • Characters: Quantity of dots, hyphens, slashes, '@' symbols, and digit ratios.
  • Security Flags: Raw IP hostnames, URL shortener domains, and high-risk TLDs.
  • Entropy: Shannon character entropy to detect algorithmically generated tokens.
STEP 02

Random Forest Classification

The 26-element feature vector is fed to a 100-tree Random Forest classifier trained on 20,000 verified legitimate and phishing URLs:

  • Ensemble Voting: 100 decision trees evaluate subsets of features independently.
  • Predictive Non-linearity: Identifies subtle correlations (e.g., authentication words inside subdomains on unfamiliar TLDs).
  • Overfitting Resistance: Bagging and random feature splits ensure high generalization on unseen URLs.
STEP 03

Calibrated Scoring & Limitations

Predictions are mapped into 3 transparent risk levels with clear acknowledgments of technological boundaries:

  • Legitimate (Green): Phishing probability < 40%.
  • Suspicious (Yellow): Phishing probability between 40% and 65%.
  • Phishing (Red): Phishing probability > 65%.
  • Safety Caveat: Machine learning identifies structural patterns; it does not guarantee content authenticity. Legitimate sites can be compromised, and attackers constantly innovate URL structures.

RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Mission & Technical Foundation

Phishing remains the primary attack vector for credential theft, unauthorized financial access, and corporate account takeovers. Traditional blacklisting methods suffer from high latency when confronting zero-day phishing campaigns whose domains often survive for only hours before being decommissioned.

PhisLens addresses this vulnerability by applying statistical machine learning directly to lexical and structural URL patterns. By training on a verified distribution of tens of thousands of legitimate and malicious URLs, PhisLens recognizes phishing characteristics in real time with zero network risk to the user.
